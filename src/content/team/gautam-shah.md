Gautam Shah founded Shah & Amin Financial Services in 2000, convinced that every family deserves an advisor who takes the time to listen, explains things clearly, and is still there years later when life changes.

He has been helping clients ever since. Over more than a quarter century in the business, Gautam has served many individuals and families — helping them protect the people they love with life insurance, build and safeguard retirement savings through annuities, IRAs and 401(k)s, and find the right health insurance and Medicare plans. He also guides clients through long-term care and disability income insurance, and much more.

His approach is simple: listen first, explain every option in plain English, and treat every family like his own. Whether you are planning for retirement, comparing Medicare options for the first time or making sure your family is protected, Gautam will help you understand your choices — and make them with confidence.
